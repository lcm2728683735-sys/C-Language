My Best Friend
I have a best friend named Lily.
We met when we were in primary school, and we have been close ever since. 
Lily is kind, helpful, and always ready to listen. 
Whenever I face difficulties, she encourages me and helps me solve problems. 
We study together, play sports, and share our dreams. 
I feel lucky to have such a wonderful friend, and I believe our friendship will last forever.
